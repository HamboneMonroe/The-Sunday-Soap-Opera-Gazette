By Barry Lee Competent | League Beat Writer & Chief Front Office Investigator

Week 9 didn't just shake up The Sunday Soap Opera—it practically tore the stadium down and salted the earth beneath it. In a week of pure apocalyptic mayhem, the high-flying West division monarch suffered his second loss of the year after being contaminated by bad tartar sauce, a perfectly synchronized front office dropped a historical 222-point orbital strike, and a front office in Steeltown traded away the #3 overall Superflex quarterback for a pocketful of loose buttons and a rusty bicycle.

WEEK 9 GAME RECAPS

MATCHUP OF THE WEEK: Danger Cats 125.05, Whopper Wentzday 114.55

In our official marquee heavyweight clash presented by Kalshi, the Danger Cats pulled off the ambush of the decade, felling Whopper Wentzday 125.05 to 114.55 to hand the West leader his second loss of the season.

  • Danger Cats Key Performers: C. Watson (WR): 23.10 pts || T. Higgins (WR): 15.20 pts || K. Cousins (QWRT): 13.80 pts || J. Gibbs (RB): 13.40 pts
  • Whopper Lowest Performers: J. Goff (QWRT): 3.00 pts || M. Evans (WR): 2.97 pts || J. Downs (WR): 2.40 pts || Patriots DEF: 2.00 pts
MATCHUP OF THE WEEK: THE KING IS TOXIC! Whopper Wentzday's empire collapses under a biohazardous 3.00-point Superflex disaster from Jared Goff as the Danger Cats strike.

Whopper Wentzday marched into Sunday strutting around like fantasy royalty, only to suffer the most embarrassing fast-food blowout in league history. Superflex starter Jared Goff forgot how to throw a football, putting up a putrid, unmoored 3.00-point line that smelled like expired tartar sauce. The Danger Cats pounced on the greased-up carcass, getting 23.10 points from Christian Watson and a gutsy team effort to send the King crying into his paper crown. The felines move to 5-4-0, proving even 7-1 front offices can crumble under catastrophic decision-making.

Clench Tuxedos 222.90, RUN CMC 113.15

In the single most terrifying offensive massacre ever recorded by this publication, Clench Tuxedos turned RUN CMC into fine dust in a historic 222.90 to 113.15 slaughter.

  • Clench Tuxedos Key Performers: C. Stroud (QWRT): 49.20 pts || J. Love (QB): 40.90 pts || K. Walker III (RB): 32.30 pts || P. Nacua (WR): 21.00 pts || Bears DEF: 20.00 pts || J. Bates (K): 18.00 pts
  • RUN CMC Lowest Performers: G. Smith (QB): 5.05 pts || B. Allen (RB): 2.40 pts || N. Collins (WR): 4.80 pts

Operating as a computer-calibrated, hyper-efficient unit, the Bi-Managers constructed a lineup so lethal it should be banned under international treaty. C.J. Stroud (49.20) and Jordan Love (40.90) combined for nearly 90 points at quarterback alone, while Kenneth Walker III ran for 32.30 and Puka Nacua added 21.00. Poor RUN CMC actually had Christian McCaffrey put up 20.70 points, but Geno Smith’s pathetic 5.05-point line made them look like a squashed squirrel on the highway. The Tuxedos advance to 5-4-0 with the highest scoring total in the entire league, leaving the rest of the East shivering in fear.

White Bronco 171.35, The Pain Train 115.00

White Bronco wiped off last week’s mud and launched an absolute cruise-missile attack on The Pain Train, rolling to a 171.35 to 115.00 beatdown.

  • White Bronco Key Performers: B. Purdy (QB): 29.60 pts || D. Prescott (QWRT): 23.85 pts || C. Lamb (WR): 23.50 pts || Packers DEF: 20.00 pts || J. Williams (RB): 18.40 pts
  • The Pain Train Lowest Performers: A. Jeanty (RB): 2.00 pts || J. Waddle (WR): 2.20 pts || K. Pitts Sr. (TE): 2.30 pts

Any rumors that White Bronco was losing their edge were erased in violent fashion. Brock Purdy (29.60), Dak Prescott (23.85), and CeeDee Lamb (23.50) incinerated the secondary, leaving The Pain Train derailed in a ditch. Train manager could only watch in total despair as Ashton Jeanty delivered a microscopic 2.00 points, dropping the Train to 3-6-0 while Bronco moves into a tie for first place at 7-2-0.

Marr Burrow Smokes 148.60, Steeltown Junkies 88.25

Marr Burrow Smokes treated Steeltown Junkies like an unranked high school team, cruising to a ruthless 148.60 to 88.25 execution.

  • Marr Burrow Key Performers: J. Burrow (QWRT): 37.25 pts || C. Schwesinger (D): 16.50 pts || C. Williams (QB): 15.55 pts
  • Steeltown Lowest Performers: M. Washington Jr. (RB): -0.30 pts || M. Gesicki (TE): 1.10 pts || T. Watt (D): 0.00 pts (BYE)

Joe Burrow shredded the defense for 37.25 points to keep the Smokes flying high. Meanwhile, Steeltown Junkies’ management reached new heights of negligence by starting T.J. Watt while he was on an obvious Bye week (0.00) and getting a comedy-gold negative -0.30 from M. Washington Jr. Marr Burrow sails to 6-3-0 in the West, while Steeltown plummets to 4-5-0.

Josh Almighty 109.15, Kyler Duty 89.25

In a grinding, mud-soaked defensive slugfest, Josh Almighty out-ugly'd Kyler Duty 109.15 to 89.25.

  • Josh Almighty Key Performers: K. Fairbairn (K): 20.00 pts || T. Lawrence (QWRT): 19.00 pts || Browns DEF: 16.00 pts
  • Kyler Duty Lowest Performers: T. Kraft (TE): 0.90 pts || Ravens DEF: 1.00 pts || T. Kelce (TE - BN): 13.30 pts

Josh Almighty didn't even need Josh Allen (12.20) to show up, thanks to kicker Ka'imi Fairbairn booting an absurd 20.00 points. Kyler Duty committed pure front-office sabotage by benching Travis Kelce (13.30) for Tucker Kraft (0.90). Almighty glides to 6-3-0, while Kyler Duty rots at 2-7-0.

Three Dots an a Dash 115.30, Autobots 103.75

Pop the champagne and throw confetti in the streets! Three Dots an a Dash delivered a glorious, heroic triumph, knocking off the Autobots in a 115.30 to 103.75 stunner.

  • Three Dots Key Performers: C. Little (K): 21.00 pts || K. Concepcion Jr. (WR): 15.80 pts || J. Daniels (QB): 14.30 pts
  • Autobots Lowest Performers: J. Dobbins (RB): 2.20 pts || C. Godwin Jr. (QWRT): 2.80 pts || J. Williams (WR): 4.00 pts

The Autobots mob—a moniker this very publication bestowed upon them after their second ruthless trade heist of the season—thought they could breeze into Sunday shaking down the division cellar. Instead, TDnD rose up like absolute legends to capture their SECOND win of the year! The syndicate managed the impossible: wasting a majestic 25.65 points from Shedeur Sanders and 22.50 from Ja'Marr Chase because their supporting enforcers operated like they were wearing concrete boots. TDnD didn't care—they let kicker Cam Little blast 21.00 points through the uprights and watched the mob scramble back to their hideouts in total shame. Both squads now stand at 2-7-0, but TDnD owns every single piece of bragging rights!
