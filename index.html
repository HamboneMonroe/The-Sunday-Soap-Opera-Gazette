EDITORIAL DESK: ARE WE BUYING THE WHITE BRONCO HYPE, OR ARE THEY DESIGNED TO REMAIN CURSED?

By Barry Lee Competent

At 9-2-0 with 1,628.40 points scored and a 70-point public flogging of Josh Almighty fresh in the rearview mirror, White Bronco is currently parading around the East like they already booked the engraver for the championship trophy. On paper, it is easy to see why the bandwagon is overflowing: Dak Prescott (47.15) and Brock Purdy (29.75) are operating like a two-headed cheat code, Bo Nix is lounging on the bench as luxury Superflex insurance, and CeeDee Lamb, Drake London, Chris Olave, Javonte Williams, and Tucker Kraft give them legitimate firepower across the board.

Hold your horses, though—before we crown them, let’s remember whose nameplate is bolted to the hood. This is White Bronco, a franchise historically engineered to step on a garden rake the exact nanosecond the playoffs begin! Look beneath that shiny 177.50-point hood in Week 11: their entire offense lives and dies on Dak and Purdy combining for 75 to 80 points every Sunday, while their supporting skill slots—Jaylen Warren (2.90), Adonai Mitchell (3.00), and Tyler Warren (2.20)—did little more than get their cardio in. When the cold December winds blow and one of those quarterbacks inevitably turns back into a pumpkin in Week 15, there is no bell-cow RB1 safety net waiting to catch them. Until they actually survive a postseason Sunday without choking on a hairball, this beat writer is enjoying the show—with one hand firmly on the curse alarm.
