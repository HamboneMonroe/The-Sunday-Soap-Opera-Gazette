Purdy and Prescott Spark White Bronco Blowout; Whopper Wentzday Hangs 191 on Autobots

By Barry Lee Competent | League Beat Writer

Two weeks into the season, early divisional standings are beginning to take shape across The Sunday Soap Opera. With the bottom two franchises in each six-team division set to be excluded from the postseason and relegated to the Toilet Bowl Tournament—where the consolation prize is the #1 overall pick in next spring's draft—the early pressure on underperforming rosters is mounting.

Here is the breakdown of Week 2 action across the league, followed by the Week 3 matchup previews.

Week 2 Game Recaps

White Bronco 170.00, Danger Cats 131.10

In the marquee matchup of the week, White Bronco capitalized on its 10-pick stash in the top five draft rounds to secure a 38.90-point victory over Danger Cats.

  • White Bronco Key Performers: Brock Purdy (QB): 35.35 pts | Dak Prescott (QWRT): 38.55 pts | DeVonta Smith (WR): 18.70 pts
  • Danger Cats Key Performers: Jahmyr Gibbs (RB): 17.30 pts | Stefon Diggs (WR): 16.70 pts | Justin Herbert (QB): 7.80 pts
WEEK 2 MARQUEE MATCHUP: White Bronco outduels Danger Cats in a high-scoring thriller.

The combined 73.90 points from Purdy and Prescott proved overwhelming for Danger Cats. Danger Cats dropped to 1-1 after starting Justin Herbert over a bench that included Deshaun Watson (25.10 points). White Bronco moves to 2-0 with 302.60 total points forced. For a franchise with a long, painful history of failing to even make the playoffs, sitting near the top of The Steeltown Junkies East feels like uncharted territory—leaving the rest of the league waiting to see if this is a genuine breakthrough or just setting up an even more hilarious late-season collapse.

Whopper Wentzday 191.30, Autobots 109.95

Whopper Wentzday posted the highest single-week team score of the season, dominating Autobots in an 81.35-point landslide.

  • Whopper Wentzday Key Performers: Jared Goff (QB): 37.05 pts | Jaxon Smith-Njigba (WR): 34.50 pts | Drew Lock (QWRT): 29.75 pts | Brandon Aubrey (K): 18.00 pts
  • Autobots Key Performers: CeeDee Lamb (WR): 27.30 pts | Ja'Marr Chase (WR): 19.50 pts | Aaron Rodgers (QB): 0.85 pts

Whopper Wentzday's explosive dual-QB output and nearly 20 points from kicker Brandon Aubrey left Autobots completely behind. Autobots dropped to 0-2 following a zero-touchdown week for several starters, highlighted by Aaron Rodgers finishing with 0.85 points. Missing draft night and handing the wheel to the autodraft algorithm rarely yields gold, and an 81-point thrashing was pure comedy for arch-rival Josh Almighty, who takes immense pleasure in watching Autobots get dismantled. Autobots now sits in the cellar of the Whopper Wentzday West at 0-2.

Josh Almighty 164.65, Clench Tuxedos 124.15

Josh Almighty maintained its spot atop The Steeltown Junkies East with a decisive victory over Clench Tuxedos.

  • Josh Almighty Key Performers: Josh Allen (QB): 48.30 pts | Davante Adams (WR): 32.50 pts | Jonathan Taylor (RB): 25.20 pts
  • Clench Tuxedos Key Performers: Patriots Defense (DEF): 27.00 pts | Tyler Shough (QWRT): 23.90 pts | Quentin Johnston (WR): 1.40 pts

Josh Allen led all scorers across the league with 48.30 points, supported by heavy outputs from Davante Adams and Jonathan Taylor. Through two weeks, Josh Almighty paces the league with 386.70 points forced. Clench Tuxedos falls to 0-2 in year two of the current co-management regime, leaving the team struggling inside The Steeltown Junkies East Toilet Bowl line despite holding extra early draft capital entering the season.

Rivalry Matchup: Steeltown Junkies 127.35, Three Dots an a Dash 100.60

In one of the league's most heated traditional rivalries, defending champion Steeltown Junkies claimed bragging rights over Three Dots an a Dash in a 26.75-point Steeltown Junkies East battle.

  • Steeltown Junkies Key Performers: Kirk Cousins (QWRT): 26.75 pts | Amon-Ra St. Brown (WR): 26.20 pts | Tre Tucker (WR): 18.90 pts
  • Three Dots an a Dash Key Performers: Jayden Daniels (QWRT): 17.70 pts | Broncos Defense (DEF): 11.00 pts | Jaxson Dart (QB): 1.00 pt

These two franchises regularly play for a side-bet case of beer—a wager that Three Dots an a Dash now owes, though Steeltown Junkies’ notorious history of conveniently failing to deliver after losing means Three Dots an a Dash might take its time handing it over. Having traded away its entire inventory of picks in rounds 1 through 5, Steeltown Junkies ran out a paper-thin lineup that held together just long enough behind Kirk Cousins and Amon-Ra St. Brown. Three Dots an a Dash suffered from a 1.00-point performance at starting QB from Jaxson Dart and dropped to 0-2.

RUN CMC 139.10, The Pain Train 109.45

RUN CMC improved to 1-1 by pulling away from The Pain Train in a 29.65-point Whopper Wentzday West contest.

  • RUN CMC Key Performers: Devin Lloyd (D): 27.90 pts | Harrison Butker (K): 20.50 pts | James Cook III (RB): 19.90 pts
  • The Pain Train Key Performers: Patrick Mahomes (QWRT): 36.80 pts | Jake Ferguson (TE): 16.30 pts | Carson Wentz (QB): 4.75 pts

Operating with its signature low-activity management style, RUN CMC produced a balanced lineup backed by defensive linebacker Devin Lloyd's 27.90 points and kicker Harrison Butker's 20.50 points. The Pain Train dropped to 1-1 despite a 36.80-point effort from Patrick Mahomes, as starting QB Carson Wentz managed just 4.75 points.

Kyler Duty 93.35, Marr Burrow Smokes 67.55

Kyler Duty secured a defensive victory over Marr Burrow Smokes, holding the opposing offense under 70 total points.

  • Kyler Duty Key Performers: Matthew Stafford (QB): 36.25 pts | Travis Kelce (TE): 16.10 pts | Baker Mayfield (QWRT): 12.00 pts
  • Marr Burrow Smokes Key Performers: Joe Burrow (QWRT): 17.25 pts | Texans Defense (DEF): 11.00 pts | Caleb Williams (QB): 5.10 pts

Known across the league for struggling to set a complete lineup for 17 consecutive weeks, Kyler Duty submitted a full roster and caught Marr Burrow Smokes on an ideal night. Marr Burrow Smokes laid a season-low 67.55 team points as starting quarterbacks Caleb Williams (5.10 points) and Joe Burrow (17.25 points) failed to generate momentum.
